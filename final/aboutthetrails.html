About The Trails

I founded the Pacific Trails Resort about ten years ago with a great degree of success. My name is Hector Ramirez and I'm the proud owner of this resort. My goal for starting this resort was simple, create a place where those who seek to find peace can actually obtain it. This business is unique in the sense that I'm the only permanent employee as the other people who work here are volunteers.
